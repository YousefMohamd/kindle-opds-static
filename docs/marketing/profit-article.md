## What it teaches (3–5 lines)

1. Telegram earns money only after you build a **targeted, loyal audience**
   with regular, valuable content — the channel comes first, the money
   comes months later (its table estimates 2–4 months to the first result
   for a channel + Telegram Ads).
2. Its 8 methods are: a specialised channel + the official Telegram Ads
   programme (50% of ad revenue, needs 1,000+ subscribers on a public
   channel), paid channels, affiliate marketing, selling products/services,
   selling ad space, paid bots, consultations/courses, and brand deals.
3. For RliBook, the methods that fit are the **official Telegram Ads**
   programme (once the mandatory channel passes 1,000 subscribers) and
   **affiliate / selling ad space** much later — never paid bots, never
   "pay to unlock withdrawal" schemes, which the article itself flags as
   ~99% scams.
4. The article's golden rule applies directly to RliBook: **content is king
   and patience is mandatory** — no quick profit, no get-rich promises.
   That is why the marketing plan promises no income and sells nothing.
5. Practical takeaway: grow the mandatory channel organically first
   (cross-posting in welcoming groups with admin permission, social media),
   measure with analytics, and only then consider Telegram Ads or the
   deferred donate link. Donations (Buy Me a Coffee) stay the honest,
   user-approved first income step — see `donate.md`.

## What does NOT apply to RliBook

- PPD / link shorteners for direct profit: **rejected** by the user.
- Paid bots / paid subscriptions for the catalog: the catalog is free.
- Any method that asks users to pay to withdraw or to "activate": a scam
  pattern the article itself warns about — never use it.
